Casefront Recording

Convert legal consultation recordings into structured case reports.

The recording service processes phone calls and consultation recordings, extracting key facts, timelines, and case details for attorney review.

Purpose

The recording pipeline helps attorneys:

Upload consultation recordings
Generate AI-powered summaries
Extract key facts and timelines
Create structured intake reports
Reduce manual note-taking
